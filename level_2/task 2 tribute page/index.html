Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was one of the most respected scientists and the 11th President of India. He inspired millions of young minds through his vision, humility, and dedication to the nation.

I admire Dr. Kalam for his simplicity, positive attitude, and love for students. Despite achieving great success, he remained humble and always encouraged youth to dream big and work hard to achieve their goals.

"Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."
